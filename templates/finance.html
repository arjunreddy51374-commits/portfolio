    METHODOLOGY

    What these calculations do—and do not—say

    Formula and interpretation notes
    • Growth: (current revenue − prior revenue) ÷ prior revenue. Not defined when prior revenue is zero or negative; period definitions must match.
    • Margins: gross profit, operating profit, or net income ÷ revenue. Classifications can differ across reporting standards and firms.
    • Liquidity: working capital = current assets − current liabilities; current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities; quick ratio subtracts inventory. These are point-in-time measures and do not establish cash availability.
    • Leverage: debt ÷ equity. A zero or negative equity denominator makes the usual interpretation unreliable; zero is not calculated.
    • Cash-flow quality: operating cash flow ÷ net income. One period is not a trend, and working-capital changes or one-off items can affect it.
    • Cash-flow subtotal: operating + investing + financing flows. It is not necessarily the reported net change in cash because FX and other reconciliation items may be absent.

03 / DATA QUALITY & LIMITS

Check before interpretation

    KPI DEFINITIONS

    Numerator, denominator, exclusions

    Operational definitions (confirm locally)
    • Average length of stay: inpatient days for the discharge cohort ÷ discharges in the same cohort/period. Agree how partial days, transfers, and observation stays are handled.
    • Bed occupancy rate: occupied staffed bed-days ÷ available staffed bed-days × 100. The denominator must use staffed/available capacity for the same facility and dates.
    • Consultation wait: total elapsed wait minutes ÷ included completed consultations. Define start/end events, appointment type, cancellations, and outliers.
    • Discharge turnaround: total minutes from the locally defined discharge decision to bed release/ready ÷ included completed discharges. A common start/end definition must be agreed before comparing units.
    • Reporting completeness: reports received ÷ reports expected × 100 for the same reporting cycle and geographic/facility scope.

    These are generic demonstration definitions, not official NHM, hospital, or NGO targets. No trend, comparison, or performance judgement is possible without validated repeated-period data and approved definitions.
